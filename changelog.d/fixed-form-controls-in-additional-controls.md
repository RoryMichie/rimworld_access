Form controls on a mod's own dialog now reach you complete. Three things were going wrong at once in the "Additional controls" section: a control whose label was only a character or two was dropped entirely, a control with a short label was dropped whenever that word happened to appear inside something else on the screen, and a control drawn with its name beside it arrived with no name at all. Progression: Education's class dialog showed all three, losing its subject picker and its class hours outright and leaving its class name field, skill picker and classroom picker unnamed. Nothing is dropped now unless we are certain it is already spoken elsewhere.

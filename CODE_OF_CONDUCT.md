# Code of Conduct

This is a small project built by and for people who were locked out of games they wanted to play. The whole point of it is to make something more welcoming, so the one rule here is simple: do not make this space less welcoming than the game it opens up.

## In practice

- Be decent to people. Argue about code and design all you want, but do not attack the person.
- No harassment, slurs, or demeaning behavior, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or the Discord.
- Remember that the people here are volunteers spending their own time. Act accordingly.
- Accessibility is the entire purpose of this project. Hostility toward disabled people, in any form, ends your participation immediately.

## Reporting

If someone is being a problem, tell me. The fastest way is a direct message to the maintainers on the [Discord](https://discord.rimworldaccess.com). Reports are handled privately.

## Enforcement

I maintain this project and I have the final say on who takes part in it. Breaking the spirit of the above can get your contributions declined, your issues closed, and your access removed, without a drawn-out appeals process. This is a hobby, not a courtroom.
